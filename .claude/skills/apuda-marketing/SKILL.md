# ApuDa Medical Content Marketing

Use the search-demand, topical-cluster, distribution, and measurement ideas adapted from the MIT-licensed `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` `content-strategy` guidance. Upstream revision and license: `docs/reviews/2026-11-10-seo-geo-marketing-skills.md`.

## Brand and audience

- Brand: **ApuDa / 아프다**. Where branded text is designed, prefer the established blue brand color. Tone: warm, calm, precise, hopeful without promising cure.
- Primary readers: people diagnosed with cancer, caregivers, and families seeking understandable, sourced education.
- Sources: existing public 30-cancer FAQs, `암 진단 후 첫 30일` books, approved video scripts, and primary medical evidence. This skill does not certify those sources as clinically reviewed.
- Channel goals: useful discovery → comprehension → navigation to appropriate existing content, not fear-based engagement or unsafe medical conversion.

## Workflow

1. Inventory existing ApuDa content before proposing a new topic. Avoid creating a near-duplicate of one of the 3,000 FAQs or an existing 10-episode series.
2. Build one topic cluster around a real patient question (e.g., lung cancer treatment-selection factors). Map a source-backed FAQ, book section, short video, longer YouTube/blog explanation, and concise Instagram/TikTok description to the same evidence record.
3. For the ApuDa 60-second series, write six *10-second* story beats. Prioritize dynamic medically grounded anatomy, tissue and molecular biology, movement, and camera work; do not default to repeated physician portraits. A clear 57–59-second Korean narration target is a production planning value, not a text-only guarantee—verify the actual TTS audio.
4. Prepare production-ready narration **without** `CUT01 |` prefixes in the copy-only output; retain cut IDs only in metadata. Preserve 16:9 generated source → 9:16 subject-safe crop and subtitle/logo postprocessing.
5. For YouTube and blog, provide a detailed source-backed description generally around 3,800–4,200 Korean characters (never exceed the platform limit specified by the user); for Instagram and TikTok, use brief faithful adaptations. Keep claims aligned across channels.
6. For proposed content calendars, label topic, target patient question, existing source, differentiation, platform, review status, owner/next action, and measurable objective. Distinguish drafts from approved/published assets.
7. Propose KPIs such as organic impressions, Search Console CTR, page engagement, clicks to existing FAQ/book, video retention and completion. Use actual analytics only if connected; never invent baseline, traffic, growth or conversion.

## Medical, privacy and publication gates

- Verify primary evidence and exact medical claim before distribution; AI persona review does not equal named clinician approval.
- No individualized treatment instructions, emergency triage disguised as marketing, unverified survival benefit, fear tactics, fabricated testimonials or personal patient stories.
- Do not repurpose patient notebook entries, school/essay records, account data, private messages or credentials for marketing.
- Do not modify live YouTube/Instagram/TikTok, Search Console, Netlify or the ApuDa production site without separate explicit authorization.
- Report copyright/music/footage permissions and human review status for each publishable asset.
